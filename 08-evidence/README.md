# Evidence

Store sanitized screenshots that support completed practical work.

Suggested naming:
- `01-index-and-ingestion.png`
- `02-query-and-results.png`
- `03-event-details.png`
- `04-investigation-timeline.png`
- `05-detection-test.png`

Before committing:
- Remove credentials, tokens and personal information.
- Mask sensitive internal addresses or identifiers where appropriate.
- Ensure the screenshot is from your own lab.
- Link each screenshot from the relevant report.
- Do not add screenshots of example material as if they were your own results.
